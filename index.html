If Machine Works

If Machine Works is a web design and development studio focused on bridging the gap between digital and physical media. While print design tools are highly refined, web design remains dominated by developer-centric tools and workflows. Our goal is to bring design-driven thinking into the digital space, creating unique web experiences and products.

Positioned at the intersection of digital materiality, physical computing, and AI, our studio aims to push the boundaries of how the web can feel, function, and inspire.

XKCD Search Engine

A search engine for XKCD Comics.

XKCD Comics is a comic series search engine tailored for an audience of programmers and tech enthusiasts. Recognizing that many readers engage with comics through a lens of logic, structure, and creativity, we designed the platform to reflect a developer-oriented experience. The result is a platform that merges the dynamic world of comics with the functionality of a developer’s workspace, bridging two creative cultures through thoughtful design and interaction.

Made in collaboration with Aryaman Gupta

Internet Landlords

Who owns the internet? Who runs the massive infrastructure that bestows our conveniences? This website is the result of a comprehensive research around the history and the formation of clouds, data center and internet landlords. This website documents the research materials and the notes in addition to a narrative that unfolds throughout the scroll to unravel the outcomes.
