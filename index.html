If Machine Works

IF Machine Works is a web design and development studio focused on bridging the gap between digital and physical media. While print design tools are highly refined, web design remains dominated by developer-centric tools and workflows. Our goal is to bring design-driven thinking into the digital space, creating unique web experiences and products.

Positioned at the intersection of digital materiality, physical computing, and AI, our studio aims to push the boundaries of how the web can feel, function, and inspire.

Internet Landlords

Who owns the internet? Who runs the massive infrastructure that bestows our conveniences?

This website is the result of a comprehensive research around the history and the formation of clouds, data center and internet landlords.

This website documents the research materials and the notes in addition to a narrative that unfolds throughout the scroll to unravel the outcomes.

XKCD Search Engine

A search engine for XKCD Comics.

XKCD Comics have a primary audience of programmers. The design of the website took this into consideration and exposed the inner workings and data flow occuring between the client and server and used it as design elements to structure the website.
